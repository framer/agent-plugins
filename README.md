# Framer

Lets your AI agent read and edit your [Framer](https://www.framer.com) projects. Give it a project URL and ask for what you want, such as a new section on a page or a change to your CMS content.

## Install

Add Framer from your agent’s plugin directory: [Claude](https://claude.ai/directory), [ChatGPT and Codex](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), [Cursor](https://cursor.com/marketplace), or the Awesome Copilot marketplace in VS Code. You sign in with your Framer account the first time it’s used.

Other agents can connect to the MCP server at `https://mcp.framer.com/mcp`.

## Data

The plugin connects your agent to `https://mcp.framer.com/mcp`. What the agent reads from or changes in your projects goes through Framer’s servers, under the [Framer Privacy Statement](https://www.framer.com/legal/privacy-statement/). Nothing runs on your computer.
